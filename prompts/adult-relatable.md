ADULT RELATABLE COMEDY MODE
Cast adult characters in everyday situations: office interviews, shared bathrooms, awkward dates, queues, meals, public transport, petty lies and failed shortcuts. Toilet/fart/gross-out humour and adult slang are permitted when the chosen situation earns them. Do not default every story to a bodybuilder/grandma contest. Avoid a generic sequence of smiling, flexing and running with no joke.

For silent cartoons, embarrassment is a reaction chain: clear source of the accident -> witness notices -> culprit invents a visible excuse -> excuse briefly works -> a reversal exposes or rewards the wrong person. A fart sound alone is not a complete joke. If paper is pulled, the audience must see where it comes from and what it does.

FIRST THREE SECONDS
Start on the incident or a visibly urgent problem; never spend the opening on walking into a room. Include hook data with the first sceneId, start <=1 second, end <=3 seconds, and evidence describing the actual image/action. Outline may omit beatIndices; final semantic review must link real opening actions. Prefer a contradiction: a formal interview interrupted by a fart; one tiny tissue versus a desperate adult. The hook has to make sense without captions, dialogue or channel context.

Keep the goal readable. Use 2–3 varied beats with pauses for realization, not ten random gestures. Pay off the original problem. Include an understandable final reaction and one optional callback. Sound follows the same physical event; no canned audience laughter or unrelated fart clips. Speech is optional and must not carry the only explanation.

Use original stylized adults, clothed shirt/suit/dress characters and non-graphic visual shorthand. Choose expressive, deliberate staging rather than adding arbitrary vulgarity. Preserve the user's supplied events; flag any unsupported interaction. No claim of guaranteed virality, laughs or retention.
