# Two original reference-informed production modes

silent-sfx: original pencil slapstick. Muted stage, selectively coloured story objects and costumes, expressive reaction close-ups. dialogue MUST be []. No narrator, intelligible TTS or spoken sound words. Angry scolding is an annoyed face, brows, pointing, short CC0 nonverbal grunts. Punches use real impact/whoosh recordings, never a voice reading “Dishoom”. Use sound role=nonverbal for grunts. Budget silence before a reveal.

dialogue-toon: original coloured cartoon sets and characters, not a recolour of the pencil face. artStyle=toon, varied skin tones, faces, hair, clothing. Short character-to-character lines, no narrator. Every speaking actor needs its own user-selected Cartesia voice ID. Use gestures on the stressed word, a listener reaction, then a brief pause. Do not say that a bundled nonverbal preview is generated Cartesia speech.

## Story-to-screen contract
Before directing, extract explicit visual requirements into requirements[]: sceneId, characterId, propId, exact color, exact physical label, worn=true for headwear, action for actual use. Do not invent extra requirements. A red branded cap is a cap prop with red color and exact label, attached to the wearer's head. A Pepsi can is type can, blue, label Pepsi, placed and picked/drunk. Drinking requires kind=drink targeting that can, not an idle action renamed “drinks”. Costume superhero uses a blue suit, red cape/boots and chest emblem. Ordinary shirt labels use character.shirtLabel. Heat uses pose.sweat plus a strain expression and a believable fanning gesture. Keep the same design IDs, colours and ownership across shots.

Supported drink action: cup/can/bottle, hand=left/right. Actor must already hold it or be close enough to pick it first. It raises the container, aligns its lip to the mouth, tilts, then returns. amount=.3..1 controls consumed stock. Do not overlap conflicting arm performances. cap and can labels are drawn on the object, not floating captions. Use safe drawing nodes for other objects. Unsupported actions/rigs must be blocked with the concrete missing capability. Never promise arbitrary natural-language stories will always render perfectly.

Cast: design each unrelated human with a distinct combination of hairStyle, faceShape, skin, outfit, headWidth/headHeight, build=slim/average/broad/round, and accessories. Retain explicit user traits. Unrelated humans with identical design signatures are rejected and must be redesigned during outline repair. Intentional twins may share an explicit identityGroup. Do not force different clothes onto identical twins when twins were requested. Dog, cat and lion have separate animal rigs; other animals require an explicit custom design and supported action rather than pretending the human rig is that species.

## Motion and delivery
Author perform keys: readable anticipation, fast contact, 2–3 frame contact hold, delayed head/torso recoil, damped recovery, then a deliberate still reaction. Avoid continuous shaking or sine waves across every body channel. Fast actions need enough frames to read; long reaction holds may be still. Keep feet planted and use IK reach limits. Never animate a held prop with a free motion track. Punch perform must declare contactAt and accent=impact; sync the recording to that exact beatIndex/contact. Do not put unrelated fart/laugh tracks under every action.

Camera: alternate clear full-body staging, object close-up, speaker gesture and listener reaction; exclude actors only intentionally. Put reaction cuts after cause, not before. A reveal must be visible before its explanation. Respect the first-three-second hook and the requested ending. One understandable visual reversal is better than six unrelated jokes. Review original sourceIdea against actual assets, attachments, actions and sound cues; summaries and intent text are not evidence of execution.


# V8 motion and sound direction
New characters use rigVersion=2: head rotation does not move the shoulders. Head local[0,-405], hip[0,-160], neck=hip+[0,-164], shoulders=neck+[0,24]. Hands are articulated and follow forearm angle, while grips follow the held prop. Do not solve unreachable actions by stretching bones.
Use kind=punch with targetActorId for another cast member, or to for a world contact point; hit=false means a miss and must have no impact noise or victim recoil. Generic punch contact is at42%, not swat64%. It supplies wind-up, contact hold, body follow-through and spring recoil automatically. Move actors into reach first. kind=scold supplies an asymmetric point/palm/fist rant; it must use a nonverbal actor-bound sound cue. No intelligible TTS for an angry gibberish rant.
Use curated sound category punch/whoosh/gibberish/mutter/steps/plop/cloth when applicable. Do not pick a random boing, gulp or grunt simply to fill silence. Punches have a short pre-contact air-swish and an exact contact impact. A miss has only the swish. Actor-bound nonverbal cues carry actorId and role=nonverbal; measured sound energy drives the mouth. Genuine gibberish recordings replace repetition of the same single grunt.
Reference-informed delivery: 150–300ms anticipation, 70–140ms strike, 2–3 frame readable contact hold, delayed head reaction, brief deadpan pause. Do not speed all channels uniformly. Reaction close-ups should make the face occupy roughly half to three quarters of a vertical frame: zoom3–5 when appropriate, focus on the actual head. Wider action shots must show the hand and target. A close-up is a deliberate composition, not a random zoom.
For an ownership change use prop.attachmentEvents=[{at,attachment:null},{at,attachment:{actorId,bone,offset}}]. A detached prop starts from the actual prior bone world position; free motions may control it only while detached. Match trajectory arrival to the new attachment point. Preserve the prop world size through the handoff.
Silent output defaults to60fps, coloured dialogue output30fps; the plan can select24/30/60. Remotion scene Sequences use the same absolute seconds as the sound clock. Springs are for damped recoil/secondary motion; never use spring overshoot to miss a required hand contact.
