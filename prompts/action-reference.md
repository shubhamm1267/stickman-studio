ADDITIONAL TESTED ENGINE PRIMITIVES
- Backgrounds office and bathroom are procedural. chair/toilet/door are placed behind actors. chair/toilet origin is at the seat rim; typical seat y680, feet floor y780. Adult seated initialPose: head[0,-350],hip[0,-100],leftFoot[-55,0],rightFoot[60,0]. wardrobe shirt/suit and accessory glasses/moustache are character data. No external asset needed.
- fart: short anticipation/shudder; visible non-graphic green puff starts at20% of duration and drifts to beat.to WORLD destination. amount controls puff size. Pair SFX syncTo=contact with beatIndex. For gross-out jokes show who caused it and who reacts. Do not emit a cloud in unrelated stories.
- nosePinch: selected hand moves to actual head/nose, remaining there until a later hand action. scoot: temporary seated side-to-side weight shift, body returns to its original root; contact SFX at35%. panic: temporary shake with widened face.
- paperRoll: a wall roll with a dangling sheet. pullPaper needs targetId paperRoll and to WORLD hand endpoint. It draws a ribbon from the roll to the clamped wrist and spins the roll. Final hand must be within arm reach. wrap needs targetId paperRoll; amount 0..1 controls persistent procedural bandages on the actor. It is an explicit comic paper-wrap effect, not arbitrary cloth simulation. Place wrap after a visible paper pull. Amount0 removes bandages.
- openDoor requires a door target. Reaches the handle, then opens a stylized hinged panel; hand tracks the handle. Close by setting a new scene placement open0; arbitrary door physics are unsupported.
- Effects and paper ribbons are renderer primitives, not just intent labels. Use them only for matching story events. Camera must keep source object and consequence readable before a reaction close-up.

- tearFree requires targetId paperRoll. It removes the paper wrap and ribbon over its duration and emits procedural paper confetti. Use after an actual paper wrap; it does not simulate arbitrary cloth tearing.

V6 PERFORMANCE ACTION
Use kind="perform" to author one continuous piece of acting instead of adjacent unrelated custom beats. Supply keyframes:[{at:0,pose:{}},{at:0.3,pose:{rightHand:[90,-350]},emotion:"strain",rightHandShape:"open"},{at:1,pose:{rightHand:[60,-130]},emotion:"defeat"}]. Keys must strictly increase from0 to1, at most24 keys. Each numeric pose channel interpolates independently from its prior key; missing channels hold. Each key's timing smooth/snappy/gentle controls the incoming segment. Emotion/handShape switches at the key, so hold an emotion long enough to read it. Do not claim the engine synthesizes in-between drawings or understands intent.
Pose fields include headScale:[x,y] in .7..1.3, eyeOpen:0..1, mouthOpen:0..1.3, blush:0..1, handScale:.7..1.5, and leftWristAngle:-180..180 alongside wristAngle for the right wrist. These give deliberate squash, blinks, mouth holds and hand silhouettes. Keep mouthOpen near1 when not deliberately animating it. headScale changes head artwork, not limb lengths or world contact targets. Hands now render in front of a face when they cross it; arms remain behind the face.
Optional leftHandShape/rightHandShape = fist/grip/point/open at a beat or performance key. Do not leave fingers in a pointing pose after the gesture ends. Both left/right attached hand props follow wrist rotation.
A perform beat may have contactAt:0..1. Sound cues with beatIndex and syncTo:"contact" use exactly start+duration*contactAt. Contact sound without contactAt is rejected. Put an explicit pose key at that instant; geometric checks cannot invent or certify contact for you.
swat may set beat.to to a fixed WORLD strike point. Use this for a missed swat: the insect may move away while the palm continues to the original cheek. Without to, swat follows its target. Do not leave an invisible target marker to stand in for a physical action. The self-slap example uses real fixed cheek points and one visible insect prop.
Performance key times and explicit contact instants are included in geometry inspection; unreachable key poses block rendering. A camera actorIds exclusion also hides that actor's owned accessories, preventing floating masks or headphones.

V6.1 COSTUMES, FLIGHT AND EVENT PROPS
Character costume:"superhero" draws a reusable muscular blue/selected-outfit suit, chest emblem, cape, coloured legs and boots. Optional capeColor and bootColor select colours. Set muscle around.8..1.1 in pose. This is a procedural costume, not a separate video service. Ordinary characters keep their existing rig.
pose.position is a WORLD root coordinate, unlike local head/hip/hands/feet. It can be keyed in perform to fly or descend without walk gait. A horizontal flying example uses bodyAngle:90,headAngle:-90,head:[-76,-405],rightHand:[0,-565],leftHand:[-60,-300],hip:[0,-160]. Keep the arms in reach. Cast root y780 can be paired with initialPose.position at sky height; set shadow:false for a distant flight. Landing keeps the original floor shadow plane.
A placed prop may have appearAt and disappearAt in scene seconds. The renderer hides it outside that interval; do not use offscreen teleports to fake an emitted object. A future bone attachment may specify attachAt. Before that time the prop follows free motions; from that time it follows its attachment. Placement scale is relative to the attached actor for the entire scene, so size does not jump on attachment. End the free track at the exact attachment point/time.
Free prop motions accept timing:"linear" as well as default smooth motion. Arc shapes the trajectory, positive lifting its middle. A small positive arc with a lower final point can make a readable accelerating cartoon drop. This is authored motion, not general fluid physics.
Dialogue may use fixture only for a trusted bundled voice in assets/sfx/manifest.json; live new dialogue still uses Cartesia. A spoken line animates the mouth while preserving the actor's underlying angry/calm expression. Do not treat arbitrary filenames as voice fixtures. The supplied hero demo includes generated original Hindi speech with explicit source records.
For comic punches use perform with fist hand shapes, explicit windup/contact/retract keys and contactAt. Match the receiver's recoil to that exact event and check actual world hand coordinates. A short impact star can use the prop visibility interval. Keep the participants and contact on camera; do not replace the contact with a summary or offscreen effect.


V9 staging: Set beat.motionEffect="flight" on any perform beat depicting flight (including hover); this emits moving wind lines and directs a CC0 air bed. Animate world root and body lean/cape, not a static floating pose. Free falling objects use motions.timing="gravity" (quadratic acceleration) and impactOnEnd=true for landing particles and a synchronized plop. Use arc=0 for gravity drops. Punch uses targetActorId, actual cheek contact at 42%, foreground impact and separate whoosh/contact sounds; hit=false is a miss and gets no contact sound or star. Pick/place/drink reach hands and forearms stay foreground while crossing a face; never hide a reaching hand with foreground prop layers. Frame rate is chosen by the user and must not change seconds or joke timing. Auto duration: allocate readable anticipation/action/reaction/payoff beats without padding. Avoid explaining the punchline in dialogue; build a simple visual setup and a causal reversal. Use sparse meaningful gibberish turns; pause the mouth between actual recorded phrases.

For custom props define propDesign.gripOffset=[x,y] in object coordinates at a visible edge/handle. Pick uses that exact grip point and preserves the offset during carry. Do not put a grip in the centre of food or through the face.


GROUND CONTACT AND GRIPS: An upright human cannot pick/place a ground object merely by extending a hand. World ground y780 is roughly 300 local pixels below the shoulder; arms reach only 200 local pixels. Before picking OR placing on the floor, animate a crouch with custom pose hip:[0,-50], head:[0,-275], feet kept on the ground; then pick/place while crouched. Use stand only AFTER the floor contact is complete. Place.to is the world OBJECT center, not a hand position. For objects with gripOffset, pick and place use the same local gripOffset (rotated and scaled with the object). Keep targets horizontally near the shoulder. A generic reach is a successful contact, not a failed attempt: use custom/perform for a deliberate miss and show the free object moving away. Every requirement with propId and action needs that exact targetId even for slip/react/gestures; omit action for outcomes implemented through motions/attachments.

HEAD ATTACHMENTS: attachment.offset is local to the selected bone, NOT actor root. The head already sits at local y=-405: an item on its crown normally uses offset:[0,-70], never [0,-405] or [0,-420]. Compute a free landing motion endpoint as actor root + actor scale*(head local + crown offset); for an upright scale1 adult at [270,780], crown is approximately [270,305]. End the free motion exactly at attachAt/event.at so no ownership overlap or teleport occurs. Keep the landing and final head prop visible in the camera, and carry prop/actor state across consecutive scenes.
