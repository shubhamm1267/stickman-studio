V5 PENCIL CARTOON DIRECTION
Human characters default to artStyle=pencil: big uneven oval eyes, bulging cheeks, elastic drawn jaw, small rounded nose, sparse ink hair, thin limbs and black hands. Use original character designs. The goal is readable drawn poses and timing, not tracing a reference. Keep quiet paper-colour sets with muted clothing and a few saturated story objects. Do not turn every background/object white.

Each short needs a clear contrast in the first 1–3s, a simple desire, a causal failed solution and a stronger reversal. For original ideas, privately compare three different mechanisms before choosing. Avoid mechanically repeating fart -> surprised face. For gross-out jokes, give the character a mistaken belief or a practical cover-up, establish why it fails, then let another character reverse the situation. Surprise can come from information hidden behind a real prop; the reveal must actually be animated, not just written in summary. Preserve explicit user story events.

Pose -> anticipation -> fast action -> contact -> reaction hold. Let a face or silhouette carry each beat. Reserve 0.3–0.8s to read a reaction, then change the situation. Close-ups must reveal information or a facial change. Keep the active contact visible. Do not introduce long static holds to reach the requested length. Vary expressions instead of using one frozen grin. No film captions, narration or title cards unless the user enables dialogue; physical object labels remain allowed.

New props: headphones, gasMask, newspaper. Place a bone-linked prop with scene.props[].attachment={actorId,bone:'head'|'leftHand'|'rightHand',offset:[localX,localY]}. Its placement scale is relative to that actor and stays consistent if picked up later. Head attachments follow head/body rotation. For a paper held at both sides, attach it to one hand with a local offset to the paper centre; explicitly pose the other hand at the opposite edge. A physical mask hides mouth artwork but preserves eye acting. A newspaper is a solid occluder: make it large enough to cover the hidden item before lowering it. Do not make a mask appear from nowhere.

pick grips headphones at the right ear cup and transfers ownership from the head to the selected hand after contact. custom pose.wristAngle (-180..180deg) rotates a right-hand prop while keeping its grip point on the wrist. Use local wrist/head poses within arm reach; place moves a scaled/rotated held object to its WORLD centre and detaches without a jump. Throw releases at40% of the beat; later free-prop motion tracks may follow the throw, but may not overlap the driven/held phase. Explicit layer='front' occludes bodies AND hands; do not request floating hands above furniture. Ground shadows stay at floor height through jumps/rotations and may be disabled for actors inside containers.

There is no renderer branch named after a sample story. Every event must correspond to supported actions, poses, attachment state, geometry and camera shots. Unsupported interactions must be repaired or reported, never disguised by changing an intent string. These structural rules cannot guarantee a laugh or automatic artistic judgement.

V6 REFERENCE STUDY
The supplied clips use sparse paper-colour stages, dominant facial silhouettes, thin ink limbs, black palms and clean contact/reaction cuts. Keep this visual grammar with original character geometry. Avoid busy skyline linework and excessive decorative props. The main object must remain readable on a phone. Use larger hands only when a close action needs them. A squash should last a few frames, settle with one small overshoot, then hold; do not wobble every channel continuously. A deliberate half-second expression may read better than multiple arbitrary cuts. Do not claim exact reference equivalence or guaranteed comedy.
